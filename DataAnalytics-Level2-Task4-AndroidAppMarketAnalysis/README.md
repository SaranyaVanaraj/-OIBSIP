# Android App Market Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes Google Play Store data to understand the Android app market, app ratings, pricing patterns, installs, and user sentiment. The analysis uses Python and data visualization techniques to identify trends and generate insights that can help Android app developers make informed decisions.

## Objectives
- Analyze the distribution of apps across different categories.
- Examine app ratings and user satisfaction.
- Explore the relationship between app size and installs.
- Compare free and paid applications.
- Analyze paid app pricing and estimate potential revenue by category.
- Perform sentiment analysis on user reviews.
- Identify potential opportunities for Android app developers.

## Dataset
The datasets are obtained from Kaggle:

**Dataset source:** https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/lava18/google-play-store-apps

The project uses the following files:
- `googleplaystore.csv` – Contains app details, categories, ratings, reviews, installs, prices, and other attributes.
- `googleplaystore_user_reviews.csv` – Contains user reviews, translated reviews, and sentiment-related information.
- `license.txt` – Contains the dataset license information.

## Project Workflow

### 1. Data Loading and Exploration
Loaded the datasets and examined their dimensions, columns, data types, and initial records.

### 2. Data Cleaning and Preprocessing
- Identified and handled missing values.
- Removed duplicate records.
- Converted ratings, installs, and prices into appropriate numeric formats.
- Cleaned invalid rating values.
- Prepared app size values for analysis.

### 3. App Category Analysis
Examined the number of applications in different categories to understand market distribution and competition.

### 4. Rating Analysis
Analyzed the distribution of app ratings and compared average ratings across categories to understand user satisfaction.

### 5. App Size and Installs
Explored the relationship between application size and the number of installs using visualizations and correlation analysis.

### 6. Free and Paid App Analysis
Compared free and paid applications and examined the price distribution of paid apps.

### 7. Estimated Revenue Analysis
Estimated potential revenue by category using app prices and reported install counts. This is an approximation, not actual revenue data.

### 8. User Review Sentiment Analysis
Used TextBlob to classify user reviews as positive, negative, or neutral based on their sentiment polarity.

### 9. Developer Insights
Examined market saturation, average app ratings, and user sentiment by category to identify potential areas of opportunity and improvement.

## Key Insights
The analysis helps explore:
- Categories with a higher or lower number of applications.
- Differences in average ratings across categories.
- Patterns in app sizes and reported installs.
- The distribution of free and paid applications.
- Categories with higher estimated revenue.
- Positive and negative user feedback across app categories.

Specific conclusions should be based on the results generated by the notebook.

## Limitations
- The dataset may contain missing, duplicated, or inconsistent values.
- Install counts are reported in ranges or rounded values, so they may not represent exact downloads.
- Estimated revenue does not account for refunds, platform fees, subscriptions, in-app purchases, or other revenue sources.
- Sentiment analysis is based on review text and may not fully capture sarcasm, context, or complex opinions.
- The findings reflect the available dataset and may not represent the current Google Play Store market.
